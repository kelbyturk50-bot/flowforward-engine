FlowForward

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

FlowForward is a consulting practice run by Kelby Turk in Saratoga Springs, Utah. This policy explains what information FlowForward's internal tools collect and how it is used. The tools are used only by FlowForward; they are not offered to the public.

Information about businesses

FlowForward researches local home service businesses using publicly available sources: Google Maps business listings and reviews, the business's own website, and public web search results. This can include the business name, address, phone number, website, published email addresses, and the name of an owner or manager when it is publicly listed. It is used only to decide whether FlowForward may be able to help the business and to write a personal outreach email.

Gmail access

FlowForward connects to its own Gmail account (flowforward50@gmail.com) through Google's Gmail API in order to:

  • Send email that Kelby has reviewed and approved, plus up to two follow-up replies in the same thread.
  • Read incoming messages in that inbox to detect replies, meeting bookings, delivery failures and unsubscribe requests for those emails, so follow-ups stop automatically.

Email content is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone. Only short excerpts needed to log a reply (for example "they replied") are stored. FlowForward's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

People who reply or book a call

If you reply to a FlowForward email or book a call, your name, email address, and the details you share are kept so FlowForward can respond and follow up with you.

Opting out

Reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" to any FlowForward email, or write to flowforward50@gmail.com, and you will not be contacted again. You can also ask for the information FlowForward holds about you or your business to be deleted.

Where information is stored

Information is stored in a private database hosted on Railway and accessed only by FlowForward. Drafting uses Anthropic's Claude API to write emails from the public research described above. FlowForward does not sell or rent information to anyone.

Retention

Information is kept while it is useful for working with a business, and deleted on request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions: flowforward50@gmail.com.
